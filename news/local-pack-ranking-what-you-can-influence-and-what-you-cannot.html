Local pack ranking: what you can influence and what you cannot

Local pack ranking: what you can influence and what you cannot

Posted by Jesper Nissen on 2026-06-19

Three factors drive local pack placement and only two respond to effort. Proximity is set by the searcher's location and your address, and no amount of optimisation changes physics. Relevance responds to category selection, service descriptions and on-page content that matches how people phrase local queries. Prominence responds to reviews, citations and general web authority. Most local campaigns underperform because they pour effort into content while leaving the profile categories wrong and citations inconsistent across directories.
