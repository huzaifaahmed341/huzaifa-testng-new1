Hreflang mistakes that quietly cost international traffic

Hreflang mistakes that quietly cost international traffic

Posted by Jesper Nissen on 2026-07-30

Hreflang breaks silently. There is no penalty and no warning, just markets quietly served the wrong page. The failures repeat across projects: missing return tags where page A points at B but B never points back, language codes paired with regions that do not exist, and x-default either absent or pointed somewhere arbitrary. Because none of it surfaces as an error in most audit tools, it survives for years while the client wonders why the German site ranks in Austria and nowhere else.
