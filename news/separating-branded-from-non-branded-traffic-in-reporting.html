Separating branded from non-branded traffic in reporting

Separating branded from non-branded traffic in reporting

Posted by Jesper Nissen on 2026-05-28

Brand searches grow when marketing spends anywhere, not only on SEO. Reporting them together with non-branded traffic makes every campaign look successful, including the ones achieving nothing. Splitting the two in Search Console takes minutes and changes the conversation entirely: brand queries show awareness, non-branded queries show whether the site is winning demand it did not already own. The second number is the one worth paying for.
